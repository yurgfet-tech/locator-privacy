Locator Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

Locator: Universal Search Bar is an Android app published by Spare Room. This policy describes how Locator accesses, uses, and transfers data.

Device search

Locator reads the list of installed apps to find apps on your device. If you enable contact, calendar, or file search and grant permission, it reads the relevant records and files to show matching results. Locator processes this search data on your device. It does not upload your contacts, calendar, files, or installed app list to Spare Room or Google. When you choose to open a result, Android passes the item or the information needed to open it to the app that handles it. You can disable optional search sources in Locator or revoke their permissions in Android Settings.

Optional Gemini features

If you provide a Gemini API key, Locator stores it using encryption backed by Android Keystore. Locator sends the key to Google over HTTPS to authenticate model and AI requests. It also fetches public model pricing and retirement information from Google. When you ask Gemini a question, Locator sends the prompt and configured AI instructions to Google. If turn-based chat is enabled, Locator also sends earlier messages and replies as context. Locator does not attach local search results to Gemini requests.

Google handles Gemini requests under the Gemini API Terms and Google Privacy Policy. Google may use prompts and responses from unpaid API usage to improve its services. For paid API usage, Google describes limited logging for safety, security, and legal purposes.

Web searches and shortcuts

When you choose a web search or shortcut, Locator hands the query to your browser or the selected app. The destination handles that request under its own privacy policy. The connection used for a custom search engine depends on the URL you choose.

Retention and deletion

Locator keeps pins, hidden results, and preferences on your device until you remove them or clear Locator’s storage. AI conversations are held in memory for the session. You can clear the conversation in Locator and remove your saved API key in AI Settings. Clearing app storage in Android Settings removes Locator’s locally saved data. These actions do not delete data already sent to Google or to another service. Locator does not offer user accounts.

Privacy questions

Contact Spare Room through the Locator privacy contact page. Comments there are public. Please omit API keys, prompts, and personal information. Spare Room uses inquiries to respond and address privacy issues. Public posts are hosted by GitHub under its Privacy Statement.
